# Research and acceptance history

SwitchPilot grew out of a personal investigation into a handful of macOS menu
bar controls. The repository intentionally preserves that complete history,
including failed experiments, local acceptance notes and the earlier REA/Ghidra
research. This is part of the record, not a claim that every historical
experiment is still supported.

- The app source and current documentation are at the repository root.
- Historical research artifacts remain under `artifacts/`, `evidence/`,
  `tooling/` and related directories.
- Some historical notes contain local absolute paths and local signing details
  from the development machine. They are retained for authenticity and are not
  configuration required by the current app.
- No One Switch binaries, assets or distribution code are included. One Switch
  is a product of its respective owner; SwitchPilot is independent and is not
  affiliated with or endorsed by that project.
